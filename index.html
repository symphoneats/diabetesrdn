What we work on

Care built around your kind of diabetes.

Every type of diabetes asks different things of you. Your plan starts with your labs, medications, devices and daily routine, and builds from there.

Type 2 and prediabetes

Bring your A1c down with meals built around your schedule, culture and cravings.

Type 1 and insulin-dependent diabetes

Carb counting, insulin-to-carb ratios, and turning CGM data into decisions you can make at the table.

GLP-1 medications

Enough protein and nutrients while your appetite is low, fewer side effects, and keeping the muscle you've built.

Proactive care starts on your plate.

Nutrition is one of the most powerful tools you have for managing diabetes. I help with the daily details that move your numbers: breakfasts that don't spike you by 10am, afternoons without the crash, and dinners out without a night of alarms.

Your medication does its best work when what's on your plate is working with it.

What we can dig into
  • Reading your CGM trends
  • Carb counting that doesn't take over your life
  • Preventing and treating lows
  • Eating out, travel and holidays
  • Exercise without the blood sugar swings
  • Cholesterol, blood pressure and kidney health
  • Protein and nutrients on GLP-1s
  • …and more
How it works

Small changes, steady progress.

  1. 01Your first visit

    A full assessment of your history, labs, medications and how you eat now.

  2. 02Your plan

    A few realistic goals, shaped around your routines, preferences and budget.

  3. 03Follow-ups

    We review your numbers together and fine-tune as your life, meds and goals change.
